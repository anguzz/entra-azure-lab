### Microsoft 365 Licensing Notes

Quick note on this, since I always forget where its at.

To manage licenses, go to:

> [https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/subscriptions](https://admin.cloud.microsoft/#/subscriptions)

Requirements:

* You must have the **Billing Owner** or **Billing Contributor** role.

If you don't see any subscriptions:

* Verify **Products connected to:** is set to your **tenant name**, **not your personal account**.
* If needed, click **Change billing account view** and select the tenant's billing account.
* The billing account should be **Microsoft Online Subscription Agreement (MOSA)** rather than **Microsoft Customer Agreement (MCA)**.

To change license counts:

1. Select the subscription.
2. Click **Buy/remove licenses**.
3. Adjust the quantity and save.
